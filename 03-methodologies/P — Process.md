# P — Process

*Theme · Running practices · The Fundamentals Reset (Field Manual, Edition 2026)*

**Process — how the work actually gets done**

## Why now

Fundamentals decide whether you can build it. Process decides whether it ships. These six get lumped together constantly and they answer completely different questions. The distinction that matters: Scrum and Kanban manage the work. XP and Lean shape how the work is done.
