# Break things on purpose

*Principle · Part 6 · The Fundamentals Reset (Field Manual, Edition 2026)*

You understand a system when you can predict its failure. So cause the failure.

Kill the process mid-write. Saturate the connection pool. Introduce the race.

This is the operational form of [[You understand a system when you can predict how it breaks]].
