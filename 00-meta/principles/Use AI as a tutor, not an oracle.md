# Use AI as a tutor, not an oracle

*Principle · Part 6 · The Fundamentals Reset (Field Manual, Edition 2026)*

This is the meta-skill. Instead of "write me X," ask:

- *"Explain why this design is preferred over the alternative."*
- *"Quiz me on isolation levels — don't give me the answers."*
- *"Here's my mental model of B-trees; where is it wrong?"*
- *"What would break this under load?"*

You have, effectively, unlimited access to a patient senior engineer. Most people use it as a code vending machine. That's the whole difference.

**Vault workflow:** paste a concept note's *In my own words* section and ask for the holes. Don't ask it to write that section.
