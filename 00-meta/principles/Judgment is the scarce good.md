# Judgment is the scarce good

*Principle · The closing thought · The Fundamentals Reset (Field Manual, Edition 2026)*

What's being commoditized is the *production* of code. What's becoming scarce is the *judgment* about code — what to build, whether it's right, how it fails, what it costs, where the boundaries go.

That judgment has always been built out of exactly the material in the systems ladder and the process practices — the systems knowledge that tells you how things break, and the process discipline that tells you how work actually converts into shipped software. Neither of those is a tool. Both of them compound.

The developers who struggle in this era won't be the ones who didn't learn the new tools. They'll be the ones whose entire value was in the layer that got automated — and the ones who used the tools heavily without ever building the judgment to evaluate what came back.

> You're not going back to fundamentals. You're going to where the work moved.
