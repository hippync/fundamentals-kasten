# Code with the model off once a week

*Principle · Part 6 · The Fundamentals Reset (Field Manual, Edition 2026)*

Not a toy exercise — a real problem, start to finish, docs open, no autocomplete, an hour or two.

This is the Zone 2 of software: unglamorous, feels slow, and it's the thing that keeps the engine from quietly losing capacity while you weren't watching.

The reason to protect this isn't a bet that AI gets expensive — that forecast will probably be wrong, and if you hang your motivation on it, the motivation collapses when the price drops. The real reason is structural: see [[You cannot supervise what you cannot do]].
