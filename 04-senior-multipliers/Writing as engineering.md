## Why it matters now

Senior work is mostly writing: design docs, ADRs, RFCs, postmortems, the message in the pull request. Writing forces the thinking to be complete — you find the hole in the design in paragraph three, not in production. And when generation is cheap, the precise specification *is* the work.
