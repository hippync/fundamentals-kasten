# M — The senior multipliers

*Theme · Running practices · The Fundamentals Reset (Field Manual, Edition 2026)*

**The senior multipliers**

## Why now

Three skills that aren't technical but determine whether the technical work counts. They're also the ones with the steepest AI-era premium.

## Answer these

Straight from the manual. Unchecked means you can't yet answer it out loud, without notes.

- [ ] Can I write an unambiguous spec for the thing I'm about to build?
- [ ] What are the three options, what does each cost, what does each buy, and which risk are we accepting?
- [ ] Do I understand the problem well enough to know the proposed solution doesn't address it?

## Build to learn

> Write an ADR for every non-obvious decision. Context, options, decision, consequences. One page.
